The publishing application

Korean Between the Lines is the owner's publishing application for the KRstory and NEXT KIDS YouTube channels. With the owner's Google permission, it uploads finished lessons to the selected channel, schedules publication, and checks upload and publication status. Each channel has separate authorization and episode records. Production uses the owner's DGX Spark 2.

This application is for the channel owner's use. Website visitors, including children, are not asked to sign in or authorize Google access. It does not accept viewer submissions or collect children's personal information.
