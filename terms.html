What we provide

Korean Between the Lines provides short educational animations about Korean expressions and, through NEXT KIDS, concepts in current affairs, economics, IT, AI, science, and the environment. Educational content

Examples are designed to help explain everyday usage. Meaning depends on context, relationship, and tone; a one-minute lesson cannot cover every use of an expression. Characters are fictional and narration is synthetic.
